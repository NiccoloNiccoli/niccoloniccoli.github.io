RoadTrip Attack (RTA) is a targeted adversarial attack for retrieval-based image geolocalization. It frames the attack as finding an optimal distractor journey toward an attacker-chosen location, using beam search to construct a sequence of deceptive geographic targets and generate subtle perturbations that remain effective in black-box transfer settings.
